## Big ideas

- **COUNT · Count electrons.** Shapes, bond orders, cluster types and oxidation states all come from counting valence electrons and placing them.
- **TREND · Trends and their exceptions.** Size, charge and electronegativity change steadily across and down the table; the first row and the heaviest elements are where the named exceptions live.

## Practices

- **P1 · Compute.** Carry an electron count, oxidation state or energy to a number.
- **P2 · Recall.** Know the compound, the product, the structure.
- **P3 · Predict.** Use a model (VSEPR, MO, Wade, HSAB) to say what a species does.

### 15.3 Reactions, mechanisms and equilibria



- ● **IA-15.3.A** (acs-accm-inorganic:VII.C.4, acs-accm-inorganic:VII.D.1, acs-accm-inorganic:VII.C.1, acs-accm-inorganic:VI.B.3) Assign a substitution mechanism (A, D, Iₐ, I_d) and compute a rate through an outer-sphere pre-equilibrium.

- ● **IA-15.3.C** (acs-accm-inorganic:V.F.8) Use the trans effect to plan the synthesis of a square-planar isomer.

- ● **IA-15.3.D** (acs-accm-inorganic:VI.H.1, acs-accm-inorganic:VII.C.6, acs-accm-inorganic:VIII.F.3) Quantify the chelate effect and explain its entropic and kinetic origins.
  - EK: Chelates release more free ligands (entropy gain) and, once one end binds, the other is held near the metal; ΔΔG = −RT ln 10 · Δlog β.
  - P1 · drills: ia-chelate-ddg, ia-chelate-origin
- ● **IA-15.3.E** (acs-accm-inorganic:VI.B.2, acs-accm-inorganic:VII.A.2, acs-accm-inorganic:VII.C.5, acs-accm-inorganic:IX.A.2) Compute the barrier of a fluxional process from NMR coalescence.

### 15.4 Spectroscopy and measurement



- ● **IA-15.4.A** (acs-accm-inorganic:IX.D.2, acs-accm-inorganic:X.D.7, acs-accm-inorganic:X.A.3) Find Δ_o from a d–d absorption and apply the selection rules and Tanabe–Sugano reading of d–d spectra.
  - EK: For d¹, Δ_o = hc/λ_max (ν̃ = 10⁷/λ in cm⁻¹); Laporte-forbidden d–d bands are weak, spin-forbidden ones weaker; Tanabe–Sugano diagrams map band energies to Δ_o/B.

- ● **IA-15.4.B** (acs-accm-inorganic:IX.D.7, acs-accm-inorganic:I.B.5) Compute an effective magnetic moment from a measured susceptibility and infer the number of unpaired electrons.
  - EK: μ_eff = 2.828 √(χ_M T) (cgs) ≈ √(8χ_M T); spin-only μ = √(n(n + 2)).
  - P1 · drills: ia-mu-from-chi, ia-unpaired-from-chiT
- ● **IA-15.4.C** (acs-accm-inorganic:IX.D.3) Predict multiplets in multinuclear NMR from spin I and the number of equivalent coupled nuclei.
  - EK: Lines = 2nI + 1: three ¹⁹F split ³¹P into a 1:3:3:1 quartet; deuterium (I = 1) splits ¹³C of CDCl₃ into a 1:1:1 triplet.
  - P1 · drills: ia-nmr-lines, ia-nmr-intensity
- ● **IA-15.4.D** (acs-accm-inorganic:VIII.G.4, acs-accm-inorganic:IX.A.1, acs-accm-inorganic:IX.A.4, acs-accm-inorganic:V.C.1) Use an ion-selective electrode's Nernstian response, and choose a microscopy or luminescence technique.
