## Big ideas

- **POSITION · Position is the electron count.** A transition metal's group number is its valence-electron count, and group minus oxidation state is its d count. Knowing where an element sits is the first step of every electron-counting argument.
- **TRIADS · Columns, not just rows.** Chemistry runs down the groups. The 4d and 5d members of a triad are close in size because the lanthanoids sit between them.

## Practices

- **P1 · Recall.** Give a number, symbol, group or neighbour without working it out.
- **P2 · Reproduce.** Write a whole row or column from memory, in order.
- **P3 · Count.** Turn position and oxidation state into a d or f electron count.
