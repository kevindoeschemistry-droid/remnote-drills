## Big ideas

- **COLD · A structure is vocabulary.** You cannot reason about a mechanism or a pathway while reconstructing what the molecule looks like. The common structures have to come out as fast as a word does.
- **FAMILY · Learn one, vary it.** Most structures are a parent with one change (phenol is benzene with an OH; galactose is glucose flipped at C-4). Carry the parent and the change.

## Practices

- **P1 · Produce.** Draw the structure from its name on paper, with nothing to look at, in under a minute.
- **P2 · Recognise.** Name a drawn structure in a few seconds.
