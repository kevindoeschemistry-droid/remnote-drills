## Big ideas

- **COUNT · Count electrons first.** Oxidation states, d counts, half-equations and titration ratios are all electron bookkeeping. Get the count and the rest follows.
- **SPLIT · Ligands split the d orbitals.** Geometry and ligand strength set the splitting; the splitting sets spin, colour, magnetism and stability.
- **TREND · Position predicts behaviour.** Down a group ions grow and oxides turn basic; across a period oxides turn acidic. Most descriptive facts are a trend with a few named exceptions.
- **PACK · Solids are geometry.** Where atoms sit in the cell fixes how many there are, how many neighbours each has, how full the space is and how X-rays scatter.

## Practices

- **P1 · Compute.** Carry an oxidation-state, electron or lattice calculation to a number.
- **P2 · Recall.** Know the colour, the product, the test.
- **P3 · Predict.** Use a trend or a model to say what will happen.
