## Big ideas

- **ZOO · Twelve fermions and four forces.** Six quarks and six leptons in three generations, each with an antiparticle, interacting by exchanging photons, W and Z bosons and gluons.
- **CONSERVE · What can happen is what conserves everything.** Charge, baryon number and each lepton number balance in every reaction; energy decides whether an allowed reaction can actually go.

## Practices

- **P1 · Compute.** Carry a charge, a quantum number or an energy to a value.
- **P2 · Decide.** Say whether a reaction is allowed and which interaction is responsible.
