## Big ideas

- **ZOO · Twelve fermions and four forces.** Six quarks and six leptons in three generations, each with an antiparticle, interacting by exchanging photons, W and Z bosons and gluons.
- **CONSERVE · What can happen is what conserves everything.** Charge, baryon number and each lepton number balance in every reaction; energy decides whether an allowed reaction can actually go.

## Practices

- **P1 · Compute.** Carry a charge, a quantum number or an energy to a value.
- **P2 · Decide.** Say whether a reaction is allowed and which interaction is responsible.

## Unit 2 · Conservation laws and energy

### 2.1 Allowed and forbidden



- ● **PP-2.1.A** (aqa-7408-particles:3.2.1.7) Check charge, baryon number and lepton numbers in a reaction or decay.
  - EK: Each balances separately; electron and muon lepton numbers are counted apart.

### 2.2 Mass and energy



- ● **PP-2.2.A** (aqa-7408-particles:3.2.1.3) Convert a mass to a rest energy and find a pair-production threshold.
  - EK: 931.5 MeV per u; a pair costs twice the rest energy.
  - P1 · drills: pp-rest-energy, pp-pair-threshold
- ● **PP-2.2.B** Calculate a Lorentz factor and a dilated lifetime or range.
  - EK: γ = 1/√(1 − v²/c²); lab lifetime = γ × rest lifetime.
  - P1 · drills: pp-lorentz-factor, pp-muon-survival

### 2.3 Strangeness, quark changes and specific charge



- ● **PP-2.3.A** (aqa-7408-particles:3.2.1.6) Find the strangeness of a hadron and say which interactions conserve it.
  - EK: s quark −1, anti-s +1; conserved by the strong interaction, changed by one in weak decays.
  - P1 · P2 · drills: pp-strangeness, pp-delta-s
- ● **PP-2.3.B** (aqa-7408-particles:3.2.1.7) State the quark change in beta-minus decay, beta-plus decay and electron capture.
  - EK: β⁻: d → u. β⁺ and electron capture: u → d. Only the weak interaction changes flavour.
  - P2 · drills: pp-quark-change, ra-decay-chain
- ● **PP-2.3.C** (aqa-7408-particles:3.2.1.1) Calculate the specific charge of a particle or nucleus.
  - EK: Specific charge = charge ÷ mass, in C/kg.
  - P1 · drills: pp-specific-charge, pp-specific-ratio
