## Big ideas

- **SAFE · Safety first, then the rest.** Airway, breathing, circulation; acute over chronic; unstable over stable; the least invasive action that protects the client.
- **CALC · Every dose is a ratio.** Desired over have, per kilogram, per hour; units carried through and the answer checked against a safe range.
- **CUES · Findings are cues to interpret.** A lab value, a vital sign or a symptom means something only against its range and the client's condition.

## Practices

- **P1 · Calculate.** Carry an order to a dose, rate or score.
- **P2 · Prioritize.** Choose who or what comes first, and who does it.
- **P3 · Interpret.** Read a finding against its range and name what it means.
- **P4 · Know the rule.** State the standard of care.

## Unit 6 · Pharmacological and Parenteral Therapies (13–19%)

Big ideas: CALC, SAFE

### 6.1 Dosage calculation



- ● **NR-6.1.A** Calculate tablets or mL for an ordered dose.
  - EK: Desired over have, times the vehicle.
  - P1 · drills: nr-tablets, nr-liquid
- ● **NR-6.1.B** Calculate weight-based doses, divided daily doses, and check an order against a safe range.
  - EK: Pounds ÷ 2.2; mg/kg/day ÷ doses a day; compare the daily total with the range.
  - P1 · P3 · drills: nr-lb-kg, nr-weight-dose, nr-daily-divided, nr-safe-range
- ● **NR-6.1.C** Draw up a reconstituted medication from its label concentration.
  - EK: Use the mg/mL after mixing, not the diluent volume.
  - P1 · drills: nr-reconstitute, nr-reconst-dose

### 6.2 Parenteral and intravenous therapies



- ● **NR-6.2.A** Calculate pump rates, gravity drip rates and infusion times.
  - EK: mL/h = volume ÷ hours; gtt/min = mL × drop factor ÷ minutes.
  - P1 · drills: nr-iv-mlh, nr-gtt, nr-infusion-time
- ● **NR-6.2.B** Calculate titrated infusions in units/h and mcg/kg/min, both ways.
  - EK: Find the concentration first, then dose ÷ concentration.
  - P1 · drills: nr-heparin-rate, nr-heparin-units, nr-mcg-kg-min, nr-insulin-drip
- ● **NR-6.2.C** Recognise IV complications and the first action for each.
  - EK: Infiltration cool and pale; phlebitis warm and red; overload crackles and JVD; air embolism sudden dyspnea.
  - P3 · drills: nr-iv-complication, nr-iv-comp-2

### 6.3 Blood and blood products



- ● **NR-6.3.A** Decide ABO and Rh red-cell compatibility.
