## Big ideas

- **SAFE · Safety first, then the rest.** Airway, breathing, circulation; acute over chronic; unstable over stable; the least invasive action that protects the client.
- **CALC · Every dose is a ratio.** Desired over have, per kilogram, per hour; units carried through and the answer checked against a safe range.
- **CUES · Findings are cues to interpret.** A lab value, a vital sign or a symptom means something only against its range and the client's condition.

## Practices

- **P1 · Calculate.** Carry an order to a dose, rate or score.
- **P2 · Prioritize.** Choose who or what comes first, and who does it.
- **P3 · Interpret.** Read a finding against its range and name what it means.
- **P4 · Know the rule.** State the standard of care.

## Unit 1 · Management of Care (15–21%)

Big ideas: SAFE

### 1.1 Establishing priorities



- ● **NR-1.1.A** (ncsbn-nclex-rn:MOC.10) Choose which client to see first from airway, breathing and circulation, acute over chronic, unstable over stable.
  - EK: A silent chest in asthma, a pulseless limb, stridor after neck surgery and a transfusion reaction come before pain at 5/10.
  - Needs: NR-7.1.B, NR-7.2.A, NR-4.4.C, NR-8.3.B
  - P2 · drills: nr-priority, nr-priority-2

### 1.2 Assignment, delegation and supervision



- ● **NR-1.2.A** (ncsbn-nclex-rn:MOC.3) Assign a task to the least-qualified person who can do it safely.
  - EK: The RN keeps assessment, initial teaching, planning, evaluation and unstable clients.
  - Needs: NR-1.1.A
  - P2 · drills: nr-delegation, nr-delegation-2

### 1.3 Legal rights, informed consent and ethical practice



- ● **NR-1.3.B** (ncsbn-nclex-rn:MOC.2, ncsbn-nclex-rn:MOC.5, ncsbn-nclex-rn:MOC.8, ncsbn-nclex-rn:MOC.11, ncsbn-nclex-rn:MOC.13) Protect client rights and confidentiality, including in electronic records and social media, and act as an advocate.
  - EK: Share information only with those caring for the client; never post client details; access only records you need; speak up when care is unsafe.
  - Needs: NR-1.2.A
  - P4 · drills: nr-confidentiality, nr-client-rights
- ● **NR-1.3.A** (ncsbn-nclex-rn:MOC.1, ncsbn-nclex-rn:MOC.12, ncsbn-nclex-rn:MOC.14, ncsbn-nclex-rn:SIPC.6) Apply informed consent, the right to refuse, advance directives and incident reporting.
  - EK: The provider informs, the nurse witnesses; incident reports never go in the chart.
  - Needs: NR-1.3.B
  - P4 · drills: nr-legal, nr-legal-2

### 1.4 Coordinating care



- ● **NR-1.4.A** (ncsbn-nclex-rn:MOC.4, ncsbn-nclex-rn:MOC.6, ncsbn-nclex-rn:MOC.9, ncsbn-nclex-rn:MOC.16) Coordinate care: hand-off reports, referrals, case management and the right team member for a need.
  - EK: SBAR hand-off; refer to the discipline that meets the need (PT, OT, social work, dietitian); case managers coordinate across settings.

  - Needs: NR-1.4.A
