## Unit 4 · Dental materials (FK3, 8.0%)

Big ideas: MAT

### 4.1 Mechanical properties



- ● **DN-4.1.A** Compute stress, elastic modulus and Vickers hardness, and compare restoratives with enamel and dentin.
  - EK: MPa = N/mm²; E = σ/ε; HV = 1.8544 F/d².
  - P1 · drills: dn-stress, dn-modulus, dn-vickers
- ● **DN-4.1.B** (jcnde-domain:CC30) Compute flexural strength from a three-point bend test.
  - EK: σ = 3FL/(2bh²); ISO 4049 uses 2 × 2 mm bars on a 20 mm span.
  - P1 · drills: dn-flexural-strength

### 4.2 Resin composites and their polymerization



- ● **DN-4.2.A** (jcnde-domain:CC30) Compute radiant exposure, depth of cure, shrinkage, C-factor and water sorption.
  - EK: J/cm² = mW/cm² × s / 1000; depth = scraped length / 2; shrinkage = 1 − ρu/ρc.
  - P1 · drills: dn-radiant-exposure, dn-depth-of-cure, dn-shrinkage, dn-c-factor, dn-water-sorption
- ● **DN-4.2.B** Explain what each composite component does and how filler changes properties.
  - EK: Silane couples filler; camphorquinone absorbs blue light; oxygen inhibits the surface.
  - P3 · drills: dn-composite-types

### 4.3 Thermal behaviour



- ● **DN-4.3.A** Compute the marginal gap from a thermal-expansion mismatch.
  - EK: ΔL = Δα·L·ΔT; tooth about 11.4 ppm/°C.
  - P1 · drills: dn-thermal-gap

### 4.4 Cements, amalgam and adhesion



- ● **DN-4.4.A** (jcnde-domain:CC30) Explain the setting and properties of amalgam, glass ionomer, zinc phosphate and ZOE, and how etching works.
  - EK: High-copper removes γ₂; GI bonds and releases fluoride; eugenol inhibits resin.
  - P3 · drills: dn-restoratives
- ● **DN-4.4.B** (jcnde-domain:CC31) Choose the surface treatment for bonding a ceramic restoration.
  - EK: Glass ceramics: HF + silane. Zirconia: air abrasion + MDP.
  - P3 · drills: dn-ceramic-bonding

### 4.5 Alloys, gypsum and impression materials



- ● **DN-4.5.A** (jcnde-domain:CC31) Classify a casting alloy by noble-metal content.
  - EK: High noble ≥ 60% noble with ≥ 40% gold; noble ≥ 25%; silver is not noble.
  - P1 · P3 · drills: dn-noble-class
- ● **DN-4.5.B** (jcnde-domain:CC56) Mix gypsum at the right water/powder ratio and choose and handle impression materials.
  - EK: W/P 0.50 plaster, 0.30 stone, 0.24 die stone; pour alginate at once; PVS is the most stable.
  - P1 · P3 · drills: dn-gypsum-water, dn-impression
