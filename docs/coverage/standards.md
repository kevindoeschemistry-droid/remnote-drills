### Notes

- **Financial Accounting (CPA FAR)**: Units are the FAR blueprint's three areas and topics are its groups; the objectives are ours, written against the group titles (the representative tasks were not read).
- **AI Basics**: Units follow the ten modules of Harper Carroll's free Academy course. Only her module titles were readable, so the objectives under them are a house list.
- **AI Safety**: No accredited standard exists; course syllabi and lab frameworks are the closest thing.
- **Analytical Chemistry**: Topics named by IChO Appendix C (read in full), the GRE Chemistry analytical area and MCAT 5C. The ACS analytical content map was not read; objectives and their order are ours.
- **Anchor Quantities**: Values from CODATA 2022, standard atomic weights and 21 CFR 101.9. Which values to carry is a house list for units 1–4 and the SnapShot Key Numbers in Biology sheet for units 5–6. No chemistry standard for this was found; the ACS content maps have not been opened to check.
- **Basketball**: Introductory level read in full, higher levels in overview only.
- **Biology (AP)**: Read through a publisher's correlation table.
- **Chemistry Olympiad**: Topics from the USNCO bands (read in part) and IChO Appendix C (read in full), at national-exam depth; the items are a house list.
- **Chemistry (AP)**: Objectives past Unit 2 are written from topic titles.
- **Earth and Space Science**: Units follow the NGSS HS ESS disciplinary core ideas (ESS1 Earth's place in the universe, ESS2 Earth's systems, ESS3 Earth and human activity), read in part. Objectives and their order are ours.
- **Applied Engineering Mathematics**: UCI CBE 200 publishes no syllabus. The undergraduate part is anchored to fe-chemical; series, Sturm–Liouville, PDEs and transforms are past any exam spec and stand on the book.
- **Fluid Mechanics**: Topics from FE Chemical area 6, read in full. Objectives and their order are ours; statics is assumed by the spec rather than listed. The framework stops at what a one-question drill can carry: no Moody chart, pump curves or differential analysis yet.
- **Heat Transfer**: Also crosswalked to the FE Mechanical spec and UCI MAE 120.
- **INBDE (Dentistry)**: Units are the Domain of Dentistry's three clinical sections plus Foundation Knowledge area 3 (materials); objectives cite the clinical content area or FK they serve and are ours.
- **Advanced Inorganic Chemistry (Cotton & Wilkinson)**: Units follow the chapters of Cotton & Wilkinson 6e (table of contents read); the objectives are a house list on those chapters.
- **Inorganic Chemistry**: Units 1, 2 and 4 follow IChO Appendix D's factual-knowledge list (read in full); coordination and solids follow its preparation-only topics and the GRE inorganic area. Objectives and their order are ours.
- **Linear Algebra**: Units follow the chapters of Lay's Linear Algebra and Its Applications (a textbook, not a standard). A published curriculum standard has not been looked for yet.
- **Material and Energy Balances**: Topics from FE Chemical area 8, read in full. Objectives and their order are ours and follow the usual textbook sequence; unit 1 is assumed by the spec rather than listed.
- **Polymers and Materials**: Polymer topics from IChO Appendix C (c22, a5), IB S2.4 and FE Chemical 4.D; a house list on those items.
- **Mathematics K–8 (Common Core)**: Codes and wording checked against a state reprint; the CCSS site was blocked.
- **Mental Math**: Topic list and order from the UIL high-school chart. The short-cut techniques and the estimation topic are a house list. Unit 6 goes past the chart: the cover-up items follow an MIT 18.03 course note and the one-a-minute integration pace follows the MIT Integration Bee qualifier; which results belong in unit 6 is a house list. Pace comes from the UIL rules (7.5 s a problem) and the English multiplication tables check (6 s a fact).
- **Molecular Simulation**: No standard exists for this subject; triangulated from best-practice papers, textbooks and the Hayes group tutorial.
- **NCLEX-RN**: Units are the test plan's eight Client Needs subcategories (with their weights); topics group its related-content items; the objectives are ours.
- **Nuclear Science and Reactor Theory**: Reactor-theory units follow the NRC Generic Fundamentals Examination topic list (read in part). Nuclear physics and shielding follow the DOE fundamentals handbook's first module (read in part). Health-physics standards for the radiation-protection unit have not been looked at.
- **Organic Reactions: Predict the Product**: Families follow the reaction chapters of Bruice's Organic Chemistry (a textbook, not a standard; chapter titles 2-17 from memory). The reactions and substrates in each are a house list. Every product is mass-balanced against its reactant by the generator; regiochemistry and stereochemistry were checked by eye.
- **Particle Physics**: Objectives follow AQA A-level Physics section 3.2 (Particles and radiation), read in this session. The Lorentz-factor items go beyond it and rest on a university textbook chapter.
- **The Periodic Table from Memory**: Content from the IUPAC table (release note read; element list cross-checked in the verifier). Which parts to know by heart is a house decision from Kevin's organometallics course.
- **Physical Chemistry**: Textbook-shaped until the ACS content map is read. Thermodynamics and kinetics units are anchored to fe-chemical; quantum and spectroscopy have no outside anchor yet.
- **Physics (AP)**: Objective codes read for a few units only.
- **Process Design, Economics and Safety**: Topics from FE Chemical areas 13, 15, 16 and PE Chemical area 7 (read in full); objectives and their order are ours.
- **Chemical Reaction Engineering**: Topics from FE Chemical area 12, read in full. Objectives and their order are ours. Units 5–8 and the later letters of units 1–4 go beyond the FE spec on Fogler 5e's chapter order (a house list); the gaps are in the file header.
- **Separations**: Topics from FE Chemical area 10 and PE Chemical 6.B (both read in full); objectives and their order are ours.
- **Speaking and Articulation**: A scoring rubric plus Toastmasters' competency list; unit order is ours.
- **SQL**: Units 1–7 follow the sixteen topic groups of Oracle's 1Z0-071 exam, read through a restatement rather than Oracle's page. Unit 8 is a house list: no standard for analytic SQL was found.
- **Statistics**: Units follow the revised AP Statistics course (2026-27), of which only the unit list was read. The objectives under each unit are a house list.
- **Structures from Memory**: Units follow the chapter list of Bruice's Organic Chemistry (a textbook, not a standard). The amino acids follow IUPAC-IUB 1983 Table 1. Which compounds to carry in each unit is a house list; the MCAT content outline names the same families and has not been opened.
- **Swimming**: Rules and research papers; they say what is legal and why technique works, not what to learn in what order. Unit order is ours.
- **Thermodynamics and Statistical Mechanics**: UCI CBE 240 publishes no syllabus. Classical units are anchored to fe-chemical; the statistical-mechanics units stand on the books.
- **Wushu**: Competition rules and biomechanics papers; unit order is ours.
